18. Competitive context
    There is already a Philippine market for Texas-style / smoked-meat restaurants.
    For example, Scorched Smokehouse currently sells smoked USDA beef, American-style ribs, pulled pork, smoked pork belly, chicken and other smoked-meat rice plates. (foodpanda)
    Other smokehouse concepts are also leaning into Filipino + Texas fusion, including Smokehauz ni Mang Kulas, whose menu includes smoked Filipino classics such as sisig, dinakdakan, bistek, kare-kare, Bicol Express and other "Texnoy" dishes. (Smoke Hauz Ni Mang Kulasa Lipa)
    That means:
    "Texas-style + Filipino food"
    alone won't be enough to own the category.
    Masung needs something more emotionally distinctive.
